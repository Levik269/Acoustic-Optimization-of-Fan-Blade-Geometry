# Abstract 

Noise from cooling fans is an increasing concern as computing infrastructure grows. This study tests whether changes to blade geometry can reduce fan noise without losing thrust. Several 3D-printed fan prototypes, differing in blade geometry and construction, were compared with a commercial fan. The motor type and the rotation speed (3000 RPM) were held constant. Sound pressure levels were measured at three angles (0°, 45° and 90°) at 4 cm from the blades, inside a homemade enclosure lined with pyramidal acoustic foam, and thrust was measured with a laboratory scale.
